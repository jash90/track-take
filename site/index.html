How it works

From waveform
to finished cut.

Five stages, each one visible and editable in the studio. Music and lyrics drive the timing, a language model directs, image models draw, and rented GPUs do the heavy animation.

Listen

Stems with Demucs, beats and downbeats with beat_this, word-level lyric timing with Whisper. Sections come from Suno's [Verse] / [Chorus] tags.

Mac · or Vast GPU

Direct

Two bars per shot. A director (Claude Code, structured output) writes each shot's scene and motion from the lyrics, the cover art and your style.

local

Draw

One complete picture per shot, background and character together, from a character sheet and the cover. Made with the Codex image tool; ComfyUI Z-Image is the alternative.

local · Codex

Animate

Wan 2.2 14B image-to-video with 4-step LoRAs. Continuous shots are conditioned on their first and last frame.

Vast.ai · CUDA

Cut

Stop-motion stepping on twos with a forced new drawing on every beat, a downbeat camera pulse, karaoke captions. H.264 master and a small HEVC preview.

Vast GPU · NVENC
Continuous takes

Not a slideshow
of 45 pictures.

The first version cut on every shot and felt like flipping through illustrations. Now the video cuts only when the song enters a verse, chorus, bridge, breakdown or outro. In between, each clip ends exactly where the next one begins.

Keyframes drawn from each other

s09 to s14 are one take: a wide shot of the room, closer and closer on the woman at the window, then the camera pulls back out through the rain. Each keyframe is generated with the previous one attached, so the place, light and character stay the same.

  • First → last frame. The clip of a continuous shot is animated towards the next shot's keyframe (Wan's WanFirstLastFrameToVideo). The seam differs by about 5/255 per pixel on average, roughly the size of compression noise.
  • One camera move per take. The slow push-in spans the whole continuous run instead of resetting on every shot.
  • The director knows. Every shot is marked ✂ CUT or ∞ CONTINUES; continuous shots keep the place and costume, and the previous shot's motion leads into the next picture.
  • Your call. Flip any shot between cut and continuous in the storyboard; the header counts “9 cuts · 36 continuous”.
Intro: the camera travels from the house to the lit window in one take (s01 → s02), then a hard cut into verse one.
Six frames across the s10 → s11 seam in the final video, with karaoke captions. No jump.
The studio

A local studio
for the whole film.

sunomv serve opens a web app on your machine. Every step runs as a background job with a live log and can be cancelled; nothing is rented without a confirmation and a price. (The interface is in Polish for now.)

Rendering on Vast.ai

Rented by the minute,
freed when done.

Clips need a 24 GB+ CUDA GPU. sunomv rents the cheapest good card, prepares it, runs the whole job on it and downloads only the finished video. Workers run detached, so a laptop going to sleep cannot break a render.

  • Setup costs count. Offers are ranked by hourly price plus the model download on that host. Some hosts charge $40/TB, which turns a $0.36/h card into a $2 one.
  • Slow hosts never get the job. Each card first downloads 1 GB; one under 40 MB/s, or one that never boots, is destroyed and replaced from spare offers.
  • One card is usually cheapest. Every card pays its own setup, so the estimate compares 1, 2 and 4 cards before you confirm.
  • Nothing left running. Cards are destroyed after the download, on errors and on cancel.
Get started

Three commands
to the studio.

macOS on Apple Silicon or Linux, with uv, ffmpeg and Node 20+. Keyframes use the Codex CLI (ChatGPT sign-in), the director uses Claude Code, and clips need a Vast.ai account.

# engine and API
cd engine && uv sync --extra analysis --extra server --extra vast

# studio UI, served by the API
cd ../apps/web && npm install && npm run build

# open http://127.0.0.1:8770
cd ../../engine && uv run sunomv serve
